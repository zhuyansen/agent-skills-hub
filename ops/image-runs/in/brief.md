# Brief: pictures for Morning Loaf, a small bakery

Morning Loaf is a small neighbourhood bakery opening this Saturday. Make these six pictures for it.
The look is yours; the content below is not.

## 1. `poster.png`: the opening poster

Portrait, 3:4. A loaf of bread in warm morning light. The poster shows exactly these six lines of
text, spelled exactly like this (punctuation and currency signs included), and no other text:

- Morning Loaf
- Grand opening · Saturday 9 am
- 晨光面包 · 周六上午九点开业
- Sourdough £4.50 · Croissant £2.20
- 12 Elm Street, Bristol
- 前一百位顾客送一个可颂

## 2. `product.png`: the bread bag

Square, 1:1. The bakery's kraft-paper bread bag standing upright on a plain light-grey background,
soft studio light, a sourdough loaf peeking out of the top. The words "Morning Loaf" are printed on
the bag in dark brown. No other text.

## 3. `mascot-1.png` to `mascot-4.png`: the mascot, four times

Square, 1:1. The bakery's mascot is Bun: a small, round bread-roll character wearing a white chef's hat
and a red scarf.

- `mascot-1.png`: Bun, seen from the front, waves hello.
- `mascot-2.png`: Bun, seen from the side, runs to the left.
- `mascot-3.png`: Bun carries a tray of three croissants.
- `mascot-4.png`: Bun is asleep in a wicker bread basket.

All four pictures must clearly show the same character: same shape, colours, face, hat and scarf.
No text.

## Requirements

- Six PNG files in `~/work/output/` with exactly these names.
- No logos of real companies, no text other than the poster's six lines and the name on the bag.
