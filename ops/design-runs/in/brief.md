# Brief: landing page for Tidepool

Tidepool is an app that tells swimmers whether the water at their local beach is clean enough to swim in today.
Build its landing page as one self-contained file. Use the copy below exactly; the design is yours.

## Copy

**Headline:** Know before you swim.
**Subhead:** Tidepool checks the water quality at 2,400 beaches every morning, so you can pick a clean one before you pack the car.
**Primary button:** Check my beach

### Three things it does
1. **Today's reading** — Bacteria levels from the latest public sample, in plain words: good, fair, or stay out.
2. **Rain warnings** — Runoff after heavy rain is when beaches go bad. Tidepool tells you how long to wait.
3. **Nearby alternatives** — If your beach is closed, see the three closest clean ones.

### How it works
We read the samples that local health agencies publish, add rainfall and tide data, and update every beach by 6 a.m.

### Pricing
- **Free** — 3 saved beaches, today's reading.
- **Swimmer, $3 a month** — unlimited beaches, rain warnings, 7-day history.

### Footer
Tidepool is not a medical service. Data comes from public agencies and can be late. © 2026 Tidepool.

## Requirements
- One file: `index.html`, with its CSS (and any JavaScript) inline. No build step.
- No external images are required; draw, use CSS or inline SVG if you want imagery. Web fonts from Google Fonts are allowed.
- It must read well at 1440 px wide.
