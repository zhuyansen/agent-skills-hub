All our teas

Every tea we sell comes from one of three family farms in northern Fujian and is packed in Bristol within weeks of arriving. We list the harvest date on each pouch. Prices are for 50 grams, about 25 cups.

White tea

  • Jasmine Silver Needle: silver buds scented seven times with jasmine. £18.
  • Bai Mudan (White Peony): a bud and two leaves, honeyed and full. £12.

Choosing a tea

If you are new to white tea, start with Bai Mudan: it is hard to over-brew and tastes of hay, honey and dried apricot. Jasmine Silver Needle is lighter and sweeter, with the jasmine in the aroma more than the taste. Both can be brewed three or four times from the same leaf.

Not sure how much water or how hot? Read our brewing guide; the same temperatures work for white tea.
