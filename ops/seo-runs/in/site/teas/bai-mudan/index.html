Bai Mudan (White Peony)

£12.00 for 50 g · In stock · Harvest: April 2026

Bai Mudan is made from a bud and the two leaves below it, withered in the sun and shade and then dried at a low heat. Nothing else is done to it. Ours comes from the Chen family near Fu'an, who pick by hand on the first warm days of April.

Tasting notes

Hay, wildflower honey and dried apricot, with more body than Silver Needle. It stays sweet even when brewed a little too long, which makes it a good first white tea.

How to brew

Use 3 grams for 250 ml of water at 85 °C and steep for 3 to 4 minutes. The leaf gives three good infusions; add a minute each time.
