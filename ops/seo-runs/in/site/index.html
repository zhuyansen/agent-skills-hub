Small-batch loose leaf tea from Fujian

Lumen Tea buys from three family farms in Fuding and Fu'an, in the north of Fujian province, where white tea was first made. We visit every spring, taste each lot before we buy it, and pack it ourselves in Bristol in batches small enough to sell within a few months. The leaf you get was picked this year.

Our teas

We sell two teas for now, both white, and no blends. Our best seller is Jasmine Silver Needle: downy white tea buds scented seven times with fresh jasmine flowers, sweet and clean rather than perfumed. Bai Mudan, or White Peony, is a bud and two leaves, fuller and more forgiving to brew. You will find every tea, with tasting notes and brewing times, on the tea page.

How we brew it

Green and white teas burn in boiling water. Most of the bitterness people blame on green tea comes from water that is too hot or leaf left in too long. Our guide How to brew green tea gives temperatures, amounts and times for a mug, a teapot and a gaiwan, and what to change when a cup comes out bitter or thin.

Shipping

Orders placed before 2 pm ship the same working day from Bristol by Royal Mail. Delivery is free in the UK above £30. Every tea comes in a resealable foil pouch of 50 or 100 grams with the harvest date printed on the back.
