Jasmine Silver Needle

£18.00 for 50 g · In stock · Harvest: April 2026

Silver Needle is the first spring bud of the Fuding Da Bai tea bush, picked before the leaf opens. We buy ours from the Lin family in Diantou. In June the buds are layered with jasmine flowers picked the same afternoon; the flowers open overnight and the tea takes up their scent. The flowers are sifted out in the morning and the process is repeated with fresh flowers seven times.

Tasting notes

Sweet and clean, with jasmine in the aroma and a long, soft finish. There is no bitterness unless the water is too hot. The second and third infusions are often the best.

How to brew

Use 3 grams of leaf for 250 ml of water at 80 °C and steep for 3 minutes. In a gaiwan, use 5 grams for 100 ml and pour off after 40 seconds, adding 15 seconds for each infusion after that.
