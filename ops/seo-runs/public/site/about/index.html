About Lumen Tea

Lumen Tea is Mei and Tom Harding. Mei grew up in Fuzhou and spent ten years buying tea for a London importer; Tom ran a coffee roastery in Bristol. We started Lumen Tea in 2021 because the white tea we could buy in the UK was usually two or three years old by the time it reached a cup.

Where our tea comes from

We buy from three farms: the Lin family in Diantou, the Chen family near Fu'an and a cooperative of eleven growers in Panxi. None of them sells through a wholesaler. We pay before harvest, which lets them pick by hand and skip the machine-cut autumn crop. Each lot is tasted in Fujian and again in Bristol, and we turn down about one in three.

What we do not do

We do not add flavourings, sell blends or use tea bags. The only scented tea we sell is jasmine, and it is scented with real flowers over several nights, the traditional way. If a tea does not sell within six months of harvest, we give it to staff and friends instead of discounting it.

Questions about a tea or an order? Write to us; one of us answers every message within a working day.
