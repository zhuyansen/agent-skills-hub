Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Who We Are

Agent Skills Hub (agentskillshub.top) is operated by GoSailGlobal LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company with its registered office at 30 N Gould St, Ste N, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA. This notice explains what the site collects. GoSailGlobal’s company privacy policy also applies, including your rights and how to use them.

Information We Collect

We collect as little as we can:

  • Email address — Only if you subscribe to our weekly newsletter. Used only to send you the newsletter.
  • Forms you send us — If you apply as a verified creator, submit a tool, or request an enterprise demo, we keep what you enter (for example your name, GitHub username, email, company and message) to handle that request.
  • Pro membership and paid services — Your email address and the member key we issue, which we store only as a one-way hash. For a deep security audit, the repository you ask us to review.
  • Free trial searches — A daily count of trial searches per network, stored as a one-way hash of your IP address and the date. We do not store the IP address itself.
  • Payments — Handled by our payment provider. We do not receive or store your full card number.
  • Aggregate analytics — We use Plausible Analytics for privacy-friendly, cookie-free pageview counting. No personal data is collected and all data is aggregated.
  • Usage analytics — We use Google Analytics to measure page views and how the site is used. It sets first-party cookies.
  • Behavioral analytics — We use Microsoft Clarity for anonymous session recordings, heatmaps, and user-behavior insights. Clarity masks personally identifiable information (PII) such as email addresses and credentials before it leaves your browser. Clarity may set first-party cookies to associate sessions; no data is sold to third parties.

How We Store Your Data

Newsletter subscribers, form submissions, member keys and trial counters are stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL) with row-level security enabled. Member keys and trial counters are stored only as hashes. Only verified, active subscribers receive emails, and you can unsubscribe through the link in every newsletter. Clarity behavioral data is stored on Microsoft servers for up to 13 months and never shared with advertising networks.

Cookies

Plausible Analytics is cookie-free. Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (such as _ga) to tell visits apart. Microsoft Clarity may set first-party cookies (including a randomized user identifier) to stitch session recordings. These cookies do not contain your name or email, and we do not use advertising cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, install Google’s opt-out add-on, or block clarity.ms at the network level.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not run ads. We share it only with the service providers below, to run the site, or when the law requires it. Repository data displayed on this site is sourced from the public GitHub API.

Third-Party Services

  • GitHub API — For sourcing public repository data.
  • Supabase — For data storage, subscriber management, member keys and trial counters.
  • Plausible Analytics — For privacy-friendly traffic analytics.
  • Google Analytics — For usage analytics.
  • Microsoft Clarity — For behavioral analytics.
  • Resend — For sending newsletter and account emails.
  • Payment providers (such as Stripe) — For processing payments for paid services.
  • GitHub Pages — For hosting the website.

Your Choices and Rights

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time. To access, correct or delete your personal information, or for any privacy question, email hello@gosailglobal.com with the subject “Privacy request”. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have further rights, described in GoSailGlobal’s privacy policy.
