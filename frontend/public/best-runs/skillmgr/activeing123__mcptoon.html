List, then remove all but fivemcptoon skills index; mcptoon skills list; for each unwanted skill: mcptoon skills remove ~/work/input/library <name> (and mcptoon skills remove ~/work/input/risky pdf-tools-pro); then mcptoon skills sync ~/work/input/library ~/.claude/skills and mcptoon skills sync ~/work/input/risky ~/.claude/skills to prune the linksskills list only works after 'skills index'. 'skills remove' works at the source: it moves the skill folder out of the source (here ~/work/input/library and ~/work/input/risky) into ~/work/input/_archive/removed/, and the next sync prunes the links. That means the input material itself was changed, though the move can be undone. I also removed mcptoon's own skill with 'mcptoon skills remove ~/.claude/skills mcptoon', but the tool's first-run self-heal put it back on the next command. Final state: 5 wanted skills plus 'mcptoon' (6 total). Tokens: 21293.
