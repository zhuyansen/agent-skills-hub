How it was made (the session's closing message)

I made the video with the installed `motion-broll` skill. It's H.264, 1920×1080, 30 fps, exactly 15.00 s (450 frames), with no audio. I checked it with ffprobe and looked at stills from every section.

The skill is meant for adding motion graphics to a talking-head video, and the brief has no video. So I used its "full-frame cutaway" mode, which makes one standalone clip. It's one shape that keeps changing shape, never cuts, and a cursor drives each change. I used the skill's default look: warm gray background, black and white shapes, one orange accent. Because there was no video, I skipped the two steps that need one: the preview with the clips cut in and the before/after comparison page. I also skipped the skill's questions and its plan-approval step, as you asked.
