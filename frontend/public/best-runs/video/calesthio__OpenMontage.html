How it was made (the session's closing message)

I've rendered the Tidepool promo with OpenMontage, the tool installed from ~/src. It runs the stages from research to compose and renders with HyperFrames on this machine, with no API keys or paid models. The file is 15.0 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264, 450 frames. The pipeline's checks passed (all text meets WCAG AA contrast), and I looked at frames from the final file.

**What's on screen.** The five lines appear verbatim and in order:
1. **0–3 s:** "Know before you swim." over a sand dawn, with a rising sun and a teal waterline.
2. **3–7 s:** "Water quality at 2,400 beaches, checked every morning." on a dark sea-blue background, with a dotted coastline that lights up left to right.
3. **6–9.8 s:** "Good. Fair. Stay out." as green, amber and coral columns, each with a simple shape icon.
4. **9.2–12.7 s:** "Rain warnings tell you how long to wait." over falling rain. A ring empties like a timer, with no numbers, and turns teal as the rain stops.
5. **12.2–15 s:** "Tidepool." with "Check my beach." in a teal pill, above the waterline again.

There are no other words, logos or claims. Everything is drawn with code; the font is Selawik, which was already on this machine.

**Choices I made without asking, as you asked:**
- **Renderer:** HyperFrames was the only option available. The tool's other renderer, Remotion, isn't installed here.
- **Concept:** I picked the waterline idea out of three.
- **Sound:** the video is silent and has no audio track. Music was optional and no music generator is configured.
- **Web research:** I did a short search because the tool won't continue past research without a research brief. It is used only as background in the project files; none of it appears in the video.

**One thing I changed in the project:** the HyperFrames setup names the project `hyperframes`. That made `npx hyperframes` find the project instead of the actual tool, so the first render attempt failed. I renamed the project in `package.json`; nothing in OpenMontage itself was changed.

The project is in `~/work/project`: the composition is `hyperframes/index.html` with its design notes in `DESIGN.md`, plus the planning files, checkpoints and the original render. I didn't run the tool's optional publish step.
