How it was made (the session's closing message)

I made the 15-second Tidepool promo with the installed `launch-video` skill. ffprobe reports H.264 at 1920×1080, 30 fps, exactly 15.0 seconds long.

**What's in it:** the five lines of copy appear in order, word for word, with nothing else added. The background is deep blue water with a moving teal wave line.
1. 0–3s: "Know before you swim." rises in over the wave.
2. 3–6.2s: "Water quality at 2,400 beaches, checked every morning." A line of dots lights up as a sunrise glow sweeps across.
3. 6.2–9.2s: "Good." "Fair." "Stay out." land one by one as green, amber and red labels on a frosted panel.
4. 9.2–12s: "Rain warnings tell you how long to wait." Rain falls while a ring timer fills. The ring has no digits, so it doesn't suggest a wait time the brief doesn't give.
5. 12–15s: "Tidepool." with a "Check my beach." button, held for about 2.5 seconds once fully in.

I checked still frames from every scene for cut-off, overlapping or extra text. That check caught the button sitting too close to the name and then on the wave line, and both are fixed in the final file.

**Where I changed the skill's workflow:**
- **Length:** the skill's default is 30–45 seconds in 7 scenes. I followed the brief's 15 seconds and 5 lines instead.
- **No questions:** I skipped the skill's questions and its wait for approval of the scene plan. There were no brand colours, fonts or logo, and the brief leaves the look to me. So `brand.md` and `MOTION.md` record the colours and font I chose, and the video only shows the name as plain type, with no logo.
- **Export:** the skill suggests installing a tool called HyperFrames, but says to ask first. Instead I used Chromium and ffmpeg, which were already here, through a small script (`render.mjs`) that draws and captures every frame exactly.
- **One file only:** the skill also suggests a second, 4:5 version. I didn't make it because the brief asks for one file.
- **Sound:** the video has a silent audio track so it uploads cleanly, and no music.

The project is in `~/work/project`: `index.html` (the animation), `render.mjs`, `beats.md` (the scene plan), `brand.md`, `MOTION.md`, `CLAUDE.md` and the check frames in `checks/`.
