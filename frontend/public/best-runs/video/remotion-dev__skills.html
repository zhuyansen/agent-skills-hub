How it was made (the session's closing message)

I made the 15-second Tidepool promo with the installed Remotion skill and rendered it to `~/work/output/video.mp4`. ffprobe confirms H.264, 1920×1080, 30 fps and exactly 15.0 seconds. I looked at stills from all five scenes, and every line of copy is on screen, readable and in the brief's order.

The video has five scenes:

| Time (approx.) | On-screen copy | What's on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2.7s | Know before you swim. | Deep-water gradient with slow rolling waves |
| 2.7–6s | Water quality at 2,400 beaches, checked every morning. | A field of dots lighting up in a sweep, with a sunrise glow |
| 6–8.7s | Good. Fair. Stay out. | Green, amber and red pills popping in one after another |
| 8.7–11.7s | Rain warnings tell you how long to wait. | Falling rain and a raindrop inside a countdown ring that empties |
| 11.7–15s | Tidepool. Check my beach. | "Tidepool." in large type with a "Check my beach." button that gets a small tap |

- **Copy:** the only text in the video is the brief's copy, word for word. There are no logos, and the timer ring has no numbers on it.
- **Sound:** the video is silent, since the brief made audio optional.
- **Font:** the typeface is Outfit, which is downloaded from Google Fonts when the video renders. Re-rendering from `~/work/project` needs internet access.

The project is in `~/work/project`: the full video is the `Promo` composition, and each scene is also registered on its own.
