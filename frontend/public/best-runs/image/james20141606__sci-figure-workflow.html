How it went (the session's closing message)

All six pictures are in `~/work/output/`, made with the installed `sci-figure-workflow` skill. I checked each one by eye against the brief.

**How they were made**
- **Step 1:** Gemini wrote a spec for each picture using the skill's "golden template". That template is built for academic diagrams, so the specs came back with arrows, labelled boxes and captions. I kept the skill's style rules and removed everything that would add text the brief forbids.
- **Step 2:** GPT-image-2 rendered each picture at high quality. Every prompt ends with the skill's required line, "Do not simplify, follow every detail exactly."
- **Mascots:** mascot-1 was drawn first. Mascots 2–4 were made by editing it with the skill's "Keep everything the same. Only change…" prompts, so they show the same character.
- **Step 3:** Gemini also made editable SVG versions of all six, saved in `~/work/process/editable/`. I didn't open or check those SVGs.
- **Step 4** (refining by hand in draw.io or Illustrator) is a manual step, so it wasn't done.

**What's in each file**
- **`poster.png`:** A flat orange loaf in front of a rising sun, with all six lines spelled exactly as in the brief, including "·" and "£". There is no other text. The API returned it at 768×1024 instead of the 1536×2048 I asked for, so it is 3:4 but low-resolution for a printed poster.
- **`product.png`:** A kraft bread bag with a sourdough loaf at the top and "Morning Loaf" in dark brown. There is no other text.
- **`mascot-1` to `mascot-4`:** Bun waving from the front, running to the left in side view, carrying a tray of three croissants, and asleep in a wicker basket. The shape, colours, face, chef's hat and red scarf match across all four, and there is no text.

The skill requires flat style (no shadows or gradients), a white background and at most four colours. So the "warm morning light" and "soft studio light" are drawn as flat shapes, and the bag sits on white with a light-grey ground patch instead of a light-grey background.
